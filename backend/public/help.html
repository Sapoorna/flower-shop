THE DETAILS

Good to know.

Payment

Cash on delivery is currently the only payment option. Card payments are coming soon and cannot be selected. We never ask for or collect card details.

Delivery

Orders use Sri Lankan addresses and LKR prices. A delivery charge of LKR 350 is shown before you place your order. Your order starts as Pending; placing it does not guarantee a delivery date. Delivery arrangements and availability require confirmation.

Your orders

Sign in to see your order history and delivery details. For an order enquiry or a cancellation request, use the custom request form and include your order reference. Cancellation is subject to confirmation.

Flowers and gifts

Photos represent the style of each item. Natural flower colour and seasonal availability can vary. Custom gifts and garden visits begin as enquiries and are confirmed separately.
